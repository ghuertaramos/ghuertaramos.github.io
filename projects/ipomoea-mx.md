# ipomoea.mx

**ipomoea.mx** is a science communication project focused on sharing visually engaging and scientifically grounded content about *Ipomoea* and related Convolvulaceae.

The project brings together short stories, images, field observations, species highlights, and curated biodiversity records to communicate the diversity, ecology, taxonomy, and cultural relevance of *Ipomoea* in Mexico.

## Main platforms

### Instagram

Follow the project on Instagram for visual posts, reels, species highlights, and stories about Mexican *Ipomoea*.

[Visit Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ipomoea.mx/)
